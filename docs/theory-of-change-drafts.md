# Theory of Change drafts for review

Hi —

The new website's Mission page has a **Theory of Change diagram** built and ready: three connected stages (Problem → Approach → Outcome), shown as a horizontal flow on desktop and a vertical flow on mobile. The diagram only renders if those three stages are filled in in the Studio.

Below is a first-pass draft of what those three stages could say. They're written conservatively — anchored directly to the verbatim mission, vision, and origin story already on rootedcommunity.org. **They're not asserting any specific operational details**; they're restating the publicly-stated work in a 3-stage frame that funders will recognize.

Please edit anything freely. The goal is for these to be the version you and the team would be comfortable signing off on for funder-facing use.

---

## Stage 01 — Problem

> In our region, incarceration of BIPOC community members inflicts trauma far beyond any sentence. The harm reaches families, futures, and the community fabric we share — and tends to compound across generations and cycles of recidivism unless someone interrupts it.

## Stage 02 — Approach

> Rooted meets community members at every stage of the reentry and healing journey. Seven programs — from immediate-needs support to community circles — work alongside people where they are. We prioritize relationships over transactions, and we lead with the lived experience of community members who have been through the system themselves.

## Stage 03 — Outcome

> A community where system-impacted BIPOC people are home — housed, supported, in relationship, and treated as the full humans they are. Reduced recidivism is one measure. Restored humanity is the deeper one.

---

## Notes on the framing

A few things to flag in the drafts:

**"In our region"** — Stage 01 keeps geography implicit ("our region"). Could be tightened to "In King, Snohomish, and Pierce counties" if we want to be more concrete. Or expanded to "Across Washington" if the work has implications beyond the immediate service area.

**"Seven programs"** — Stage 02 names the program count explicitly. If we add or restructure programs in the future, this line needs updating; saying "our programs" instead would future-proof but loses the specificity. Easy to change either way.

**"Reduced recidivism is one measure. Restored humanity is the deeper one."** — Stage 03 ends with this two-sentence beat. It's the most editorial line in the three drafts; if it reads as too rhetorical, we can tighten.
